What is Artificial Intelligence?

Artificial Intelligence (AI) refers to the simulation of human intelligence processes by machines, particularly computer systems. These processes include learning (the acquisition of information and rules for using it), reasoning (using the rules to reach approximate or definite conclusions), and self-correction. This technology underpins various applications, from natural language processing and robotics to computer vision and recommendation systems.
